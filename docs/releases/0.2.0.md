# Workspaces as planets

- The workspace sidebar is now an island of planets. Each workspace is a small planet of its picture and stays where you drag it; the open one comes close.
- Green dotted rings show work still running, blue rings what came back new.
- The island leans on a window edge, resizes from its free edges and leaves the board the room beside it.
- Board and Plan keep their own filters: what you hide in one stays shown in the other. The board starts with Done and Planned hidden; the plan shows Planned and hides Done.

Thanks for testing. Please keep telling us what confuses you or breaks.
