# Facienda 0.5.0

- Goals is the window's other side: a map of goals, with subgoals on each goal's orbit and minigoals hanging under their subgoal, each with a box of the work it holds. Goals fold into beads on the rim until clicked, and a click on an orbit writes a new subgoal there.
- Today sits beside the map. Click minigoals to pick the day's work, or write new ones under their subgoal.
- The board and Goals are linked both ways. Every task row shows its place, a task's menu sets its goal and subgoal, and Facienda finds board work for a line for you to keep or dismiss.
- Opening a task covers the board: the latest exchanges in one column, in the group's colour, with the answer field under them. Answers can carry files, a working agent can be steered, and Done or the left margin brings you back.
- Every board row has its own tile with your last words and the agent's last answer. The agent writes what its row says, and the age under the glyph counts only while the agent waits on you. Sort a group's tasks by their latest message, or return to your own order.
- Workspaces sit on the island as rounded tiles in fixed spaces. The open one has a bolder ring and its name in a pill, and the island takes its colour.
- Groups and workspaces can be archived and restored. A single task can move to another workspace from its menu.
- Switch a task's model in its current chat, or continue in a new chat with a handover note. Fable 5.2 replaces Fable 5.1, and saved choices move over.
- A Claude task keeps running while its session waits on a background job, and its answer shows as soon as the turn ends. News you already read in Codex or the Claude app is cleared.
- The memory book above the gear opens Memory, with a card of what each workspace reads from the others.
- The Claude runner and idle helpers no longer wake for nothing, the memory embedder stays off the GPU, and the memory evidence check is cheap enough to run on every sync.
- Each workspace remembers its open task, including after a restart. Choose in Settings whether sending an answer returns to the board or keeps the task open.
- Search from the bar runs in the background and puts recent work in the current workspace first. Links in answers open on the first click, and local web pages such as mocks open in an isolated Facienda window.

The Mac app requires macOS 26 or later. Existing workspaces are migrated when opened; keep your normal library backups.
