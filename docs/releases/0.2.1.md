# A + on every card, and planned tasks that move

- Every card has a + at the end of its title that adds a task to it. The + at the end of the filter row (also ⇧⌘G) starts a new group with its first task, and ⌘N adds one to Unsorted.
- Planned tasks drag between groups on the board, just like started ones.
- A task or chat name that is cut short shows in full when you rest the pointer on it.
- Connecting Codex now lets Codex release a chat a minute after its turn ends, so the Codex app stops showing it as open in another app.

Thanks for testing. Please keep telling us what confuses you or breaks.
